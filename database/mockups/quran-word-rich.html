"to bring near in rank" — the cognate doubling قرّب + زُلْفَىٰ (39:3) intensifies: not mere proximity but elevation of standing.

Mir · related
أَزْلَفَ / أُزْلِفَ (الجنّةَ)

"to bring the Garden near" — the reward set within reach of the righteous (26:90 · 50:31 · 81:13).

Key-Term · سيناء

Sinai · Key Terms (semantic field: qurb / intercession)

In the polemic of 39:3 & 34:37, zulfā names the very function the mušrikūn ascribe to their intermediaries — proximity-of-rank to God. The Qurʾān does not deny nearness but relocates it: qurb is earned through ikhlāṣ and obedience, not brokered by idols.

The Tafsīrs · عَشرُ تفاسير · 39:3

al-Ṭabarīthe mušrikūn claim the idols bring them qurba wa manzila to God; God brands the claim a lie.
al-Zamakhsharīزُلْفَىٰ a maṣdar reinforcing يُقَرِّبُونَا ("qurbā"); the false logic of mediation laid bare.
al-Rāzīrational refutation — nearness to God is a rank attained by the soul, impossible to delegate to stone.
al-Qurṭubīthe verse uproots intercession-shirk; زُلْفَىٰ = the closeness of honour, not place.
Ibn ʿAṭiyyaزُلْفَىٰ = qurba; an accusative of purpose / verbal noun.
Ibn Kathīrathar-based: as the idolaters said — "we worship them only that they bring us nearer to God in standing."
al-Bayḍāwīزُلْفَىٰ = taqrīban / manzila; maṣdar on the meaning of يقرّبونا.
Abū Ḥayyāniʿrāb-focused: زُلْفَىٰ maṣdar in place of the absolute object, or a ḥāl — sense: qurba.
al-Ālūsīdegrees of nearness (مراتب القرب); the doubling marks an intensified, elevated approach.
Ibn ʿĀshūrmaqāṣid/semantic: زُلْفَىٰ the high station; qurb + zulfā together = nearness *as rank*.

concise glosses synthesized from the tafsīr corpus — the reading (qurba/manzila, refutation of mediation) is unanimous

Iʿrāb · الإعراب

al-Iʿrāb al-Muyassar · al-Jadwal · al-Tibyān (al-ʿUkbarī)

«زُلْفَىٰ»: مصدرٌ منصوبٌ نائبٌ عن المفعول المطلق (أي: تقريبًا)، أو حالٌ بمعنى مُقرِّبين؛ وألفُها للتأنيث مقصورة.

a maṣdar standing for the absolute object ("a bringing-near"), or a ḥāl; the final alif is the feminine maqṣūra.
