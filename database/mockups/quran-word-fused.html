زُلْفَىٰ

zulfā

nearness as elevated standing — rank, not bare proximity

root ز ل ف·pattern فُعْلَىٰ·39:3·سورة الزُّمَر

مَا نَعْبُدُهُمْ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلْفَىٰ

"We worship them only that they may bring us near to Allah in nearness of rank."

Examples · unique sensesشواهد?

the one root across the Qurʾān — each shāhid a different shade of sense

مَا نَعْبُدُهُمْ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلْفَىٰ

"…only that they may bring us near to Allah in nearness of rank."

nearness as rank — al-Zumar 39:3

وَمَا أَمْوَالُكُمْ … بِٱلَّتِي تُقَرِّبُكُمْ عِندَنَا زُلْفَىٰ

"…nor your wealth that brings you nearer to Us in standing."

merit-nearness — Sabaʾ 34:37

وَإِنَّ لَهُۥ عِندَنَا لَزُلْفَىٰ وَحُسْنَ مَـَٔابٍ

"And indeed, for him is nearness to Us and a good place of return." (Dāwūd)

favoured station — Ṣād 38:25

وَأُزْلِفَتِ ٱلْجَنَّةُ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ

"And Paradise will be brought near to the righteous."

brought near (verb) — al-Shuʿarāʾ 26:90

وَأَقِمِ ٱلصَّلَوٰةَ طَرَفَيِ ٱلنَّهَارِ وَزُلَفًا مِّنَ ٱلَّيْلِ

"…and at the approaches of the night." (the early night-hours)

portions of night — Hūd 11:114

فَلَمَّا رَأَوْهُ زُلْفَةً سِيٓـَٔتْ وُجُوهُ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟

"But when they see it near at hand, the faces of those who disbelieved are distressed."

near at hand (imminence) — al-Mulk 67:27

Iʿrābالإعراب?
al-Tibyān · al-Jadwal · al-Muyassar

«زُلْفَىٰ»: مصدرٌ منصوبٌ نائبٌ عن المفعول المطلق (تقريبًا)، أو حالٌ بمعنى مُقرِّبين؛ وألفُها للتأنيث مقصورة.

a maṣdar standing for the absolute object, or a ḥāl; final alif = feminine maqṣūra.

The Tafsīrs · 10التفاسير?
al-Ṭabarīidols claimed to bring qurba wa manzila to God — God brands it a lie.
al-Zamakhsharīزُلْفَىٰ a maṣdar reinforcing يُقَرِّبُونَا; the false logic of mediation.
al-Rāzīnearness to God is a rank of the soul — impossible to delegate to stone.
al-Qurṭubīuproots intercession-shirk; closeness of honour, not place.
Ibn ʿAṭiyyaزُلْفَىٰ = qurba; accusative of purpose / verbal noun.
Ibn Kathīrathar-based: "…that they bring us nearer to God in standing."
al-Bayḍāwītaqrīban / manzila; maṣdar on the meaning of يقرّبونا.
Abū Ḥayyāniʿrāb: maṣdar for the absolute object, or ḥāl — sense qurba.
al-Ālūsīdegrees of nearness (مراتب القرب) — an intensified approach.
Ibn ʿĀshūrqurb + zulfā together = nearness as rank; maqāṣid reading.

From the Lexica · 10المعاجم?
Lisān al-ʿArab — Ibn Manẓūr

الزُّلْفَى: القُربةُ والمنزلةُ والدرجةُ؛ وزُلَفُ الليلِ: ساعاتُه؛ وأَزْلَفَ الشيءَ: قرَّبه؛ وازْدَلَفَ: اقترب، ومنه المُزْدَلِفة.

al-zulfā: nearness, station and rank; zulaf al-layl: the hours of night; azlafa: he brought (it) near; izdalafa: he drew near — whence Muzdalifa.

Maqāyīs — Ibn Fāris

أصلٌ يدلُّ على القُرب والتقدُّم.

a root denoting nearness and advancing-forward.

Mufradāt — al-Rāghib

الزُّلْفَى: الحُظوةُ والمنزلةُ؛ التقرُّبُ بالطاعة.

al-zulfā: favour and high standing; drawing near (to God) through obedience.

Miṣbāḥ — al-Fayyūmī

زَلَفَ زُلوفًا: تقدَّم؛ والزُّلْفةُ: المنزلة.

zalafa zulūfan: he advanced; al-zulfa: the station/rank.

al-Ṣiḥāḥ — al-Jawharī

الزُّلْفةُ والزُّلْفَى: القُربةُ؛ ومنه المُزدلِفة.

al-zulfa and al-zulfā: nearness — whence Muzdalifa.

Kitāb al-ʿAyn — al-Khalīl

الزَّلَفُ: القُربُ؛ زُلَفُ الليلِ: ساعاتُه الأُوَل.

al-zalaf: nearness; zulaf al-layl: its first hours.

al-Qāmūs — al-Fīrūzābādī

زَلَفَ: تقدَّم؛ الزُّلْفَى: القُربةُ والدرجة.

zalafa: he advanced; al-zulfā: nearness and degree.

Tāj al-ʿArūs — al-Zabīdī

الزُّلْفَى: القُربى والمنزلةُ؛ وزُلَفُ الليلِ أوائلُه.

al-zulfā: nearness and station; zulaf al-layl: its early parts.

al-ʿUbāb — al-Ṣaghānī

الازدلافُ: الاقترابُ؛ سُمّيت المُزدلفةُ لازدلافِ الناس.

al-izdilāf: drawing near; Muzdalifa is so named for the people's drawing-near.

Lane — Lexicon

zulfà: nearness, a near approach; a high station or rank with another.
