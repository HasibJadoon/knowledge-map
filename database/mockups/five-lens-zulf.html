Key Term · Five-Lens

زُلْفَىٰ

zulfā

nearness as elevated standing

root ز ل ف · pattern فُعْلَىٰ · 39:3 · سورة الزُّمَر
Q 39:3 — Surat al-Zumar

مَا نَعْبُدُهُمْ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَا إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلْفَىٰ

We worship them only that they may bring us near to Allah in closeness of rank.

Range of Meaning

دَرَجةdegree, gradation

Memory Hook

Hear zulfā in zealous: the root is a forward thrust — an eager advance into nearness. Picture a courtier stepping zealously toward the throne, brought near into rank — not merely standing close.

rapprochementa drawing-near approximateto bring near eminencenearness as standing ingratiateto seek nearness — تَزَلُّف precinctsthe near approaches — المُزْدَلِفة

Near ≠ zulfā. Mere proximity is قُرب; zulfā is proximity that confers rank.

Meet أُزْلِفَتِ الجنّة (50:31) — feel the Garden brought near: the same root drawing two parties close.

١صَرْفMorphology

Root ز ل ف on the pattern فُعْلَىٰ — an ism masdar (al-Jawhari) of the قُرْبَىٰ/بُشْرَىٰ/حُسْنَىٰ family. Mamnu min al-sarf (alif al-tanith al-maqsura): no tanwin, case-endings withheld li-l-taadhdhur. Cognate azlafa = qarraba; izdalafa < iztalafa (al-Fayyumi).

٢إعرابSyntax

منصوب. Sibawayh, via Ibn Atiyya (al-Muharrar), reads masdar fi mawdi al-hal — as if مُتَزَلِّفِينَ — operator تُقَرِّبُونَا; وفيه خلاف. Equivalent cognate-masdar reading reinforces the verb by sense. Nasb sign: fatha muqaddara on the alif maqsura.

٣دلالةSemantics

Not bare قُرب. Ibn Faris (Maqayis): اندفاع وتقدّم في قرب — a thrust and advance forward into nearness; hence الدرجة والمنزلة. al-Raghib: الزلفى = الحظوة, المزالف = المراقي (the ascents). al-Jawhari: manzila bada manzila. Field: زُلَف الليل (11:114), المزدلفة.

٤بلاغةRhetoric

يُقَرِّبُونَا … زُلْفَىٰ stacks two nearness-words of different roots as tawkid manawi. Ibn Atiyya: they deemed themselves too lowly to reach God directly, so sought His creatures. al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir: the motive is shafaa plus manzila — rebutted, the angels intercede only bi-idhnihi. Intratextual: false here and 34:37 versus the genuine لَزُلْفَىٰ at 38:25, 38:40.

٥ترجمةTranslation

Rendering only as nearer to Allah bleeds out the rank dimension. Controlled loss: زلفى = nearness as elevated standing (الحظوة، المنزلة). Fuller: to bring us into closeness of rank with Allah. Classical glosses confirm قُربة ومنزلة (al-Tabari), الحظوة (al-Raghib).
