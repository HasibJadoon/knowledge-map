Whom Will You Worship? إخلاص الدين

The theological graph beneath the surah

Every node in Az-Zumar hangs off one claim: God alone is to be worshipped. Revelation grounds it; the powerlessness of intercessors and God's total sufficiency prove it; mercy keeps it open; and the Resurrection settles it as the throngs are sorted by what they served.

worship axis revelation / event mercy & salvation shirk & ruin
So what. The graph has a single root (God) and a single sink (the two gates). Two of the surah's hardest images — the soul taken in sleep (42) and the heavens folded in His right hand (67) — are not digressions: they are evidence nodes feeding the sufficiency that makes shirk irrational. Mercy is the one edge that lets a traveller cross from the left branch to the right — but only before death.
