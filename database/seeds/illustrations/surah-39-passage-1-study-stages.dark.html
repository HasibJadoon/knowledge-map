Surah 39 · Az-Zumar · Passage 1 · Ruku 1

Revelation & Pure Devotion تنزيل الكتاب وإخلاص الدين

Ayāt 1–9 · the study module, stage by stage — every layer drawn from the km_quran tables, rooted in all tafsīr and iʿrāb books.

The passage opens the surah by exalting the Book (v.1–2) and turns at once to its thesis — al-dīn al-khāliṣ, religion made purely for God — refuting the intercessors (v.3–4), parading the signs of creation (v.5–6), and contrasting the ungrateful with the devout qānit (v.7–9). Synthesis · rooted in all sources
Opens the surah by glorifying the Book (Ibn ʿĀshūr: an elegant exalting of the Qurʾān). Tanzīl is a form-II maṣdar, not a passive participle — gradual, emphatic revelation. The jumhūr (Jadwal, Daas, Tibyān, Zamakhsharī’s first face) make it mubtadaʾ; Zamakhsharī adds two faces; Abū Ḥayyān weighs the same. Might (al-ʿAzīz) and wisdom (al-Ḥakīm) ground the surah’s coming demand for pure worship. Makkan by consensus; also named Sūrat al-Ghuraf.

39:2command of ikhlāṣ · nominal + imperative
إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ بِٱلۡحَقِّ فَٱعۡبُدِ ٱللَّهَ مُخۡلِصٗا لَّهُ ٱلدِّينَ
“It is We who sent down the Scripture to you with the Truth, so worship God with your total devotion.”
Sentence structure · 2 jumal (1→M)
s1
Emphatic inna-assertion: We sent down the Book in truthnominal
s2
Imperative branched by the fāʾ: so worship God, purifying religion for Himimperative
Readings · iʿrāb (contested)
بالحقِّ: متعلق بـ(أنزلنا) jumhūr · حال من الكتاب min.
الدينَ: مفعول به لاسم الفاعل (مخلصًا) jumhūr · منصوب على نزع الخافض min.
Synthesis · rooted in all sources
Two jumlas: an emphatic nominal assertion (We sent down the Book in truth, predicate the verbal clause anzalnā), then an imperative branched by the fāʾ (so worship God, purifying religion). The books agree on the skeleton and differ only on bi-l-ḥaqq and al-dīn. Tafsīr reads it as the founding command of ikhlāṣ that the surah will defend.

أَلَا لِلَّهِ ٱلدِّينُ ٱلۡخَالِصُۚ وَٱلَّذِينَ ٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوۡلِيَآءَ مَا نَعۡبُدُهُمۡ إِلَّا لِيُقَرِّبُونَآ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ زُلۡفَىٰٓ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَحۡكُمُ بَيۡنَهُمۡ فِي مَا هُمۡ فِيهِ يَخۡتَلِفُونَۗ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يَهۡدِي مَنۡ هُوَ كَٰذِبٞ كَفَّارٞ

39:4refuting “a child” · conditional + tasbīḥ
لَّوۡ أَرَادَ ٱللَّهُ أَن يَتَّخِذَ وَلَدٗا لَّٱصۡطَفَىٰ مِمَّا يَخۡلُقُ مَا يَشَآءُۚ سُبۡحَٰنَهُۥۖ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡوَٰحِدُ ٱلۡقَهَّارُ

39:9the devout qānit · 3 jumal
أَمَّنۡ هُوَ قَٰنِتٌ ءَانَآءَ ٱلَّيۡلِ سَاجِدٗا وَقَآئِمٗا يَحۡذَرُ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَيَرۡجُواْ رَحۡمَةَ رَبِّهِۦۗ قُلۡ هَلۡ يَسۡتَوِي ٱلَّذِينَ يَعۡلَمُونَ وَٱلَّذِينَ لَا يَعۡلَمُونَۗ إِنَّمَا يَتَذَكَّرُ أُوْلُواْ ٱلۡأَلۡبَٰبِ

Ellipsis map · 1 maḥdhūf · the passage’s rhetorical pivot
ḥadhf — khabar & muʿādil of amكمَن هو عاصٍ / آلكافرُ خيرٌ أم الذي هو قانت The predicate of man (and the partner of am) is dropped — taqdīr ka-man huwa ʿāṣ / ka-ghayrih. Suppressing the contrast-pole forces the listener to supply it, magnifying the qānit by leaving the disbeliever unworthy of naming.
al-Jadwal · al-Muyassar · Daas (unanimous)
Readings · qirāʾāt
أمَّن (بالتشديد) · أمَن (بالتخفيف، قراءة نافع وابن كثير وحمزة) qirāʾāt
Synthesis · rooted in all sources
Contrasts the heedless ingrate (v.8) with the devout qānit standing the night. Qirāʾāt amman/aman; man is mubtadaʾ with omitted khabar (taqdīr ka-ghayrih), elided because the prior kāfir points to it; al-qānit = one persevering in obligatory obedience. Closes with hal yastawi alladhīna yaʿlamūn — only ulū al-albāb heed.
